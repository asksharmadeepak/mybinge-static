## Description (max 4,000 chars)

```
MyBinge turns your downloaded movies, TV shows, and personal videos into a beautiful offline media library — with posters, Continue Watching, and smart search. No subscription. No media server. No internet needed for playback.

WHY MYBINGE?
A folder full of downloads gets messy fast. MyBinge scans the folders you choose and builds a personal, Netflix-style library from your own files: poster grids, categories, favorites, watch progress, and search that understands movie titles — not cryptic filenames.

KEY FEATURES
• Offline movie library — organize downloaded movies with posters and descriptions
• TV show support — episodes grouped by season, with per-episode watch progress
• Continue Watching — resume any movie or episode where you stopped
• Smart metadata — automatic posters, overviews, and trailers from TMDB
• External storage — browse videos from USB drives and connected storage on supported devices
• Smart search — find any title in seconds
• Favorites & categories — curate collections your way
• Profiles — separate spaces for Kids and Everyone
• Subtitles — load subtitle files with your videos
• Offline playback — MKV and MP4 files play without internet

PERFECT FOR
• Movie collectors organizing a downloaded movie library
• Binge-watchers managing TV series across seasons
• Families keeping personal and travel videos in one place
• Anyone outgrowing plain file managers and video players

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add your media folders in Settings
2. MyBinge scans your files and matches posters and metadata
3. Browse your library by posters, categories, and Recently Added
4. Sign in to sync watch progress across your devices

YOUR FILES, YOUR LIBRARY
MyBinge organizes media you already own. Nothing is uploaded — your library is private and works offline. Internet is optional, used only to refresh posters, metadata, and trailers.

Free to install. No subscription required to organize and play your local media.

Guides, folder-structure tips, and free tools: https://offlinemedialibrary.com

Download MyBinge and turn your downloads into a library you love opening.
```
