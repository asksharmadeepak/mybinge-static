Metadata optimization begins with filename quality. Even strong lookup engines fail when release names include too much noise.

Prioritize title and year accuracy first, then apply secondary tags like resolution. Keep your naming rules simple and document them for everyone using the same library.

Over time, this creates cleaner search, better poster coverage, and fewer duplicate entries.
