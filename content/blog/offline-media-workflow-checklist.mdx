A maintenance checklist keeps your offline media library healthy without overthinking tools.

Use a repeatable weekly process: add files, normalize filenames, validate metadata, and remove duplicates. The consistency of this process matters more than perfect automation.

Small routines protect long-term quality and reduce time spent fixing broken matches later.
